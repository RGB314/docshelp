# Plans and Billing

## Plans

| Plan       | Price                    | Storage per member | Version history | Guests |
|------------|--------------------------|--------------------|-----------------|--------|
| Free       | $0                       | 2 GB               | 7 days          | 0      |
| Team       | $8 per member / month    | 50 GB              | 90 days         | 10     |
| Enterprise | Custom pricing           | Unlimited          | Unlimited       | Unlimited |

Annual billing gives a **20% discount** on the Team plan.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing
period. If a workspace is over the storage limit of its new plan, it becomes read-only until storage is reduced.

## Refunds

We offer a full refund within **14 days** of the first Team plan payment. After that, payments are non-refundable,
but you can cancel at any time to stop future charges.

## Invoices

Workspace owners can download invoices as PDF from **Settings → Billing → Invoices**.
