# Sharing and Permissions

## Roles

- **Owner**: manages billing, members, and can delete the workspace. Each workspace has exactly one owner.
- **Admin**: manages members and notebooks, but not billing.
- **Member**: creates and edits notes in notebooks they have access to.
- **Guest**: external collaborator with access to specific notebooks only. Guests are available on Team and Enterprise.

## Sharing a notebook

Open the notebook, click **Share**, and choose people or groups. Access levels are *Can view*, *Can comment*,
and *Can edit*.

## Public links

Admins can publish a single note as a read-only public link. Public links can be protected with a password
and set to expire. Public links are disabled on the Free plan.

## Transferring ownership

Only the current owner can transfer ownership, from **Settings → Workspace → Transfer ownership**.
The new owner must already be an Admin.
