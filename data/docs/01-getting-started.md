# Getting Started with Nimbus Notes

Nimbus Notes is a note-taking app for teams. Notes live in **notebooks**, and notebooks live in a **workspace**.

## Creating an account

1. Go to app.nimbusnotes.example and click **Sign up**.
2. Confirm your email address. The confirmation link expires after **24 hours**.
3. Pick a workspace name. You can rename it later under **Settings → Workspace**.

## Installing the apps

Nimbus Notes runs in the browser and has desktop apps for Windows 10+, macOS 13+ and Ubuntu 22.04+,
plus mobile apps for iOS 16+ and Android 11+.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- `Ctrl/Cmd + N` creates a new note.
- `Ctrl/Cmd + K` opens quick search.
- `Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + P` opens the command palette.
