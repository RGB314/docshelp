# Troubleshooting

## Sync is stuck (red slash on the cloud icon)

1. Check your internet connection and any VPN or proxy. Nimbus needs outbound access to port 443.
2. Sign out and sign back in to refresh your session token.
3. On desktop, use **Help → Reset local cache**. Unsynced offline edits are backed up to a
   `nimbus-recovery` folder in your Documents directory before the cache is reset.

## I forgot my password

Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. Reset links are valid for **1 hour**.
If your workspace uses SSO, reset your password with your identity provider instead.

## A note disappeared

Deleted notes go to **Trash** and stay there for **30 days** before being permanently deleted.
Admins can restore notes from Trash. To see older versions of a note, open **⋯ → Version history**.

## Still need help?

If these steps don't solve the problem, open a support ticket. Team and Enterprise customers get a first
response within **8 business hours**; Free plan users get community support only.
